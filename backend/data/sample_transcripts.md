title: Product Strategy That Works | guest: Shreyas Doshi | timestamp: 12:18 | url: https://www.lennyspodcast.com
The most useful product strategy makes explicit choices. A team cannot be everything to every customer. Start with the customer problem, name the trade-off, and align the roadmap to the chosen advantage. Good strategy says no to attractive ideas that do not reinforce the advantage.

title: Growth Loops | guest: Brian Balfour | timestamp: 24:10 | url: https://www.lennyspodcast.com
Sustainable growth comes from loops, not isolated tactics. A growth loop starts when an action by one user creates value or acquisition for another user. Measure each step of the loop, identify the limiting step, and improve that constraint before adding more channels.

title: User Research at Scale | guest: Teresa Torres | timestamp: 18:42 | url: https://www.lennyspodcast.com
Continuous discovery means talking to customers regularly and connecting opportunities to outcomes. Teams should test assumptions with small experiments instead of treating roadmap requests as proof of demand.

title: Marketplace Liquidity | guest: Andrew Chen | timestamp: 31:05 | url: https://www.lennyspodcast.com
Early marketplaces should focus narrowly on a use case and geography to create density. Liquidity is reached when users can reliably complete the core transaction, so track successful matches rather than vanity acquisition metrics.
